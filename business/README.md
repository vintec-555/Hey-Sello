# Hey Sello — Business Foundation Docs

**Hey Sello** (heysello.in) is an AI automation service for people with better things to do. You connect the tools you already use — Gmail, Slack, Notion, your CRM, spreadsheets — and describe a task in plain English ("Hey Sello, reply to new leads within 5 minutes and log them in the CRM"). A Claude-powered agent plans the work, runs it end to end, and keeps you in the loop. Brand voice: friendly, plain-spoken, a little playful.

These documents are the operating foundation for launch. Read them in this order:

## Documents

- **[gtm.md](./gtm.md)** — Go-to-market & launch plan. Defines who we sell to (5–50 person startups; founders, ops, sales, and customer-success people), the 3–4 wedge automations we launch with, how we position against Zapier/Make, hiring a VA, and copy-pasting into ChatGPT, the channels we'll use to get the first users, a concrete 90-day timeline, and the five metrics we live or die by.

- **[pricing.md](./pricing.md)** — Pricing strategy. A seat + usage hybrid across Free / Starter / Pro / Team tiers, with price points in USD and INR tuned for both the India and global SMB markets, what's included at each tier, the founder/early-bird deal for the waitlist, and how every price maps back to our real costs (LLM tokens, integrations).

- **[financial-model.md](./financial-model.md)** — A simple but honest Year 1 model. Every assumption is stated (waitlist size, conversion, ARPU, churn, COGS per active user, CAC), with a quarter-by-quarter revenue and cost table, gross margin, monthly burn, and the runway/funding that all of it implies. The arithmetic is shown so you can challenge it.

- **[legal-checklist.md](./legal-checklist.md)** — Launch legal & compliance checklist for an AI tool that touches people's email and data: entity choice (India Pvt Ltd vs. Delaware C-corp), Terms & Privacy essentials, DPDP Act + GDPR basics, OAuth least-privilege scopes, security basics, and what must be true before we collect PII at scale. Actionable, not legal advice.
